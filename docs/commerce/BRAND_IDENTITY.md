## Positioning
**Ship-ready templates and tools for builders who want product, not yak-shaves.**

- Indie hackers / solo devs  
- Small product studios  
- Creators automating content ops  

## Voice
- Direct, technical, no hype spam  
- “Here’s the zip, here’s the stack, here’s the price”  
- Build-in-public honest about holds and credits

- **Accent:** electric teal or amber (pick one in product covers; stay consistent)  
- **Type:** system-ui / Inter for web; monospace for code samples
