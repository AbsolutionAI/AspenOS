## 5. Formats that deliver real-world value

### Preferred
1. **Operator note** (5–8 lines): what broke, what we changed, checklist  
2. **Decision log**: Flash vs Grok, local vs cloud, when *not* to automate  
3. **Architecture sketch** (text diagram): fleet, plant edge, SoR  
4. **Eval / test pattern**: one assertion worth stealing  
5. **Tool teardown**: “used X for Y; failed at Z”  
6. **Thesis post**: Aspen/manufacturing opinion with one concrete implication  

### Allowed sparingly
- Threads (≤1/week)  
- Product spotlight (Fri slot)  
- Quote/reply with substance (daily engagement, not content quota)  

### Banned
- Empty “AI will change everything”  
- Engagement bait (“comment YES”)  
- Buying reach  
- CTA to unpublished SKUs
