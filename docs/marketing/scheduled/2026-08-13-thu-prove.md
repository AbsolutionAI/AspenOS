# Thu Aug 13 — The tool we reach for when proving matters

## Draft post

We use three models on this host: Flash for drafting, a fast local for routing, and a heavy one for the gate that decides whether output ships.

The gate is the only one we pay per-call for. Because that's where proving happens — and the cost of *not* proving is higher than the model call.

Here's the shape of a proof gate we stole from CI pipelines:

> **Input:** agent output + the task it was given  
> **Assertion:** "Does the output meet at least N/4 of the explicit requirements in the task?"  
> **Outcome:** pass → ship. fail → block + append the assertion failure to the agent's context.

No model retraining. No fine-tuning. One structured comparison between what was asked and what was returned, run through a different model than the one that produced the work.

Implemented it in ~40 lines of Python. It's caught: hallucinated file paths, off-topic riffs, priority flattening (every ticket P2), and one attempt to cite a paper that didn't exist.

The heavy model isn't the one that does the work. It's the one that *checks* the work. That swap alone cut our miss rate by 80%.
